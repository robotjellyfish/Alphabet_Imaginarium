This glyph scrambler replaces letters with randomized Unicode characters that look visually similar

Decorative use only—not a substitute for readable text. Visually similar glyphs are different Unicode characters therefore software and screen readers can treat them differently, pronounce them unpredictably, or fail to display them at all. Keep names, instructions, and accessibility-critical text in ordinary readable characters.
